Meet the Planets

Eight worlds, each with its own remarkable characteristics.

Mercury

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun. Its surface experiences extreme temperature changes.

Venus

A scorching world with a thick atmosphere. Venus is the hottest planet in our Solar System.

Earth

Our home planet, with liquid water on the surface and a diverse range of life.

Mars

The Red Planet, known for its rusty colour, volcanoes, valleys and evidence of ancient water.

Jupiter

The largest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a giant storm that has persisted for centuries.

Saturn

A gas giant famous for its striking rings made of icy and rocky particles.

Uranus

An ice giant that rotates on its side, giving it an unusual orientation as it orbits the Sun.

Neptune

A distant ice giant with powerful winds and a deep blue appearance.
